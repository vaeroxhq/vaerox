# VÆRØX Constitution (Permanent)

## 1. Identity

Independent engineering laboratory. Founder & maintainer: Mayank Sharma. Current stage: Building. Not an agency, not a startup landing page, not a freelancer portfolio, not an AI wrapper company. Public engineering lab documenting design, implementation, and evolution of production-grade software systems.

## 2. Philosophy

Engineering over marketing. Documentation over decoration. Architecture over aesthetics. Evidence over claims. Trust over hype. Unknown information is acceptable; fabricated information is not.

## 3. Design language

Dark canonical. Monochrome. Hairline borders. No gradients, glassmorphism, card shadows, neon, playful illustrations, emojis, or marketing hero graphics. Typography: Geist + IBM Plex Mono. Feels like internal engineering software.

## 4. Writing style

Use: built, implemented, measured, documented, benchmarked, validated, designed, tested, observed, investigated.
Forbid: revolutionary, cutting-edge, next generation, AI-powered, seamless, industry-leading, world-class.

## 5. Authenticity — never invent

Revenue, customers, funding, production deployments, infrastructure, metrics, latency, throughput, availability, cost — unless actually measured. If unknown: "Not Yet Documented" or "Target". No fake employees, departments, customers, funding, deployments, incidents, uptime, regions, cloud scale.
